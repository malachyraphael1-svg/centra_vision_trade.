How it is intended to work

A review-led process.

These are proposed platform steps, not currently active financial services.

01

Account setup

Clients would create and verify an account.

02

Payment review

Submitted payment details would be checked against confirmed receipts.

03

Record allocation

Any approved allocation would be documented with dates and amounts.

04

Request review

Withdrawal requests would be reviewed and paid only after approval.
